About

Nearly two decades in the classroom, across four countries.

James is an international educator and academic leader with nearly two decades of experience across Egypt, Kuwait, the UAE, and the United States, specializing in English instruction, curriculum development, and cross-cultural education. His background spans secondary and tertiary instruction, institutional reform, staff mentorship, and multicultural educational leadership.

He is currently engaged in developing AI-assisted educational frameworks focused on responsible student AI literacy, writing support, and ethical classroom integration of emerging technologies — always guided by one question:

“Does this actually serve the learner?”
